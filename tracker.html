UNDERSTANDING BMI

A SIMPLE SCREENING TOOL.

BMI compares weight with height and can be used as a general screening measure.

Below 18.5 Lower range
18.5 – 24.9 Healthy range
25 – 29.9 Higher range
30+ Higher range

BMI is only a general screening measure and does not diagnose health conditions.
